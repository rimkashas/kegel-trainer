Tips
  • Find the right muscles: they're the ones you'd use to stop gas or hold urine. Tighten, lift up and in.
  • Breathe normally — don't hold your breath.
  • Don't squeeze glutes, abs or thighs; keep them relaxed.
  • Don't do Kegels while urinating (can cause incomplete emptying / infections).
  • Stop if you feel pain and consult a doctor or pelvic floor physiotherapist.
  • Fully relax between squeezes — relaxing matters as much as squeezing.
